📋 Scoring Guide & Categories

How to Enter Data:

• Enter 0 = No/Fail (0% score)
Weight System:
• Weight 1 = 1 point
• Weight 2 = 2 points
• Weight 3 = 3 points
Response Categories:
• Declaration & Early Response
• Communication & Coordination
• Statuspage Communication
• Resolution & Post-Incident
💡 How It Works: Enter scores (0 or 1) for each criterion each month. For Statuspage items, select N/A when not applicable. The UI automatically calculates weighted scores, totals, and compliance percentages. Green = Excellent (80%+), Yellow = Good (60-79%), Red = Needs Improvement (<60%).
